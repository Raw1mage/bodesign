# EE owner (signs off the electrical design)

The named electrical engineer who reviews and signs off the C03 design definition. Until they sign, the design spec stays `draft` ("未核可;佈局凍結前需 EE 設計審查 gate"). bodesign authors the electrical truth and runs the analyzer gate, but the EE-owner sign-off is a human gate the AI never flips itself.
